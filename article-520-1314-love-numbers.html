520, 1314 and the love-number calendar

China has at least three Valentine's Days, and one of them exists only because of how numbers sound.

520 = 我爱你

Wǔ èr líng sounds close to wǒ ài nǐ, “I love you”. That's why May 20 (5/20) is a day for gifts, proposals and wedding registrations. 521 (wǒ yuàn yì, “I'm willing”) is used on 5/21.

1314 = 一生一世

Yī sān yī sì is heard as yī shēng yī shì, “one life, one world”, i.e. forever. It's a rare case where a 4 is welcome. 5201314 means “I love you forever”, and red envelopes of 520 or 1,314 are popular at weddings.
