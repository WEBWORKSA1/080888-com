Official rules (summary)

  1. No purchase necessary. A purchase does not improve your chances of winning.
  2. Eligibility. Open to individuals 18+ (or minors with guardian consent) where not prohibited by law. Void where prohibited. Organisers, staff and their households are not eligible.
  3. Entry period. Each contest runs from the 1st to the last day of the calendar month (UTC). One entry per person per contest.
  4. Judging. Skill-based. Story and photo entries are judged on originality (40%), cultural insight (30%) and presentation (30%). Auction Oracle goes to the closest guess without going over; ties go to the earliest entry.
  5. Prizes. Paid by PayPal or similar in USD, or as a digital report, within 30 days of confirmation. Winners handle any local taxes. No substitutions except at the organiser's discretion.
  6. Winners. Notified by email and announced on the 8th of the following month. A winner who doesn't reply within 14 days forfeits the prize, and a runner-up is chosen.
  7. Content. Entries must be original, lawful and free of third-party trademarks, copyrighted images or personal data of others. We may disqualify any entry.
  8. Privacy. Entry data is used only to run the contest. See the Privacy Policy.
