How much to put in a red envelope

The amount matters, and so do the digits. Here's a practical guide to 红包 (hóngbāo).

The three rules

  1. Even amounts for happy occasions (odd amounts are linked to funerals).
  2. Use 8, 6 or 9 where you can: 88, 168, 188, 288, 688, 888.
  3. Never include a 4, and use crisp new notes.

Typical amounts by occasion

Local norms vary a lot by city, family and income. Treat these as starting points in local currency units.

Etiquette

  • Give and receive with both hands.
  • Don't open an envelope in front of the giver.
  • Digital red envelopes on WeChat and Alipay follow the same number rules (8.88, 16.8, 88.88).
