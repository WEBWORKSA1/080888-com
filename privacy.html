Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026 · Applies to 080888.com

What we collect

  • Information you give us in forms: name, email, phone (optional), dates of birth, numbers you ask us to analyse, and messages.
  • Tool inputs (numbers, dates) are processed in your browser and are not sent to us unless you submit a form.
  • Cookies & similar technologies for preferences (theme, consent), analytics (Google Analytics, if enabled) and advertising (Google AdSense).

How we use it

To answer requests, deliver reports, run contests, process pledges, recruit, send newsletters you opted into, improve the site and show ads. We do not sell your personal information.

Form processing

Form submissions are delivered to our team by a third-party form relay (FormSubmit) and stored in our email system. Those processors handle data under their own terms.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your earlier visits to this and other websites. Google's advertising cookies let it and its partners serve ads based on those visits. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. If you choose “Essential only” in our banner, we request non-personalised ads.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA, CCPA/CPRA, PIPL), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, and to object to or restrict processing. Send a request through our contact form (topic: Privacy / data request).

Retention

Inquiries: up to 24 months. Job applications: 12 months. Newsletter: until you unsubscribe. Contest entries: 24 months.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13 (or 16 where applicable). We don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version here.
