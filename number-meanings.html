Chinese number meanings: 0–9 and the combinations that matter

Chinese numerology works mainly by sound. Each digit is heard as a word, so a number can read as a wish (“prosper all the way”) or a curse (“will die”). Here is how each digit and common combination is read.

The ten digits

DigitPinyin / 汉字Heard asVerdict
0líng 零Wholeness; linked to 灵 (spirit) and 良 (good). A fresh start.Positive
1yī / yāo 一Unity, being first; in phone numbers yāo ≈ 要 (will)Mild +
2èr 二“Good things come in pairs” 好事成双Positive
3sān 三Close to 生 (shēng) life, birthPositive
4sì 四Close to 死 (sǐ) deathAvoid
5wǔ 五Five Elements; also 无 (none) or 我 (I)Neutral
6liù 六流 (liú) flow: things go smoothlyVery positive
7qī 七Togetherness, rising 起; the 7th lunar month is Ghost MonthMixed
8bā 八发 (fā) prosper, get richLuckiest
9jiǔ 九久 (jiǔ) long-lasting; the emperor's numberVery positive

Lucky combinations

NumberReads likeMeaning & typical use
88发发 · 囍Double prosperity; looks like 囍 (double happiness). Wedding gifts, prices, plates.
888发发发Triple prosperity. Top-tier phone numbers, gold jewellery prices.
168一路发“Prosper all the way.” Very common in company names and prices.
518我要发“I'm going to prosper.” Popular in sales and trading.
666六六大顺Everything goes smoothly; online slang for “awesome”.
999久久久Everlasting. Anniversaries and 99/999 roses.
520我爱你“I love you.” May 20 (5/20) is an unofficial Valentine's Day.
1314一生一世“For a lifetime.” Paired as 5201314: “I love you forever.”
68 · 28 · 58 · 18路发 · 易发 · 我发 · 要发Short forms of “will prosper”. Good price endings.
0808零八零八The Beijing 2008 Olympic opening date pattern (08/08/08).

Unlucky combinations

NumberReads likeWhy it's avoided
14要死“Will die.” Floors 14, 24, 44 are often skipped.
514我要死“I want to die.”
748去死吧“Go die.” One of the worst sequences.
74 · 54 · 94气死 · 我死 · 就死“Angered to death”, “I die”, “then die”.
250二百五Slang for a fool. Never price anything at 250.
38三八A regional insult; also Women's Day (3/8) in a neutral sense.

Regional differences

Cantonese speakers (Hong Kong, Guangdong, much of the diaspora) avoid 4 even more strongly and value 8 heavily. Mandarin speakers share the same core rules and use online slang like 520 and 666 more. In Japan and Korea, 4 is also unlucky because its reading sounds like “death”. In Japan, 9 can be unlucky as well.
