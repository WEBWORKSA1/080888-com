Frequently asked questions

Lucky numbers

Why is 8 lucky?
8 (bā) sounds like 发 (fā), “to prosper”. Full guide.
Why is 4 unlucky?
4 (sì) sounds like 死 (sǐ), “death”. The same is true in Japanese and Korean readings.
Do non-Chinese people need to care?
If your customers, buyers or in-laws include Chinese speakers, yes. It affects how your prices, phone numbers, addresses and dates come across.

Tools

Is the analyzer free? Do you store my numbers?
It's free, and it runs in your browser. We only receive what you submit through a form.
How accurate is the zodiac finder?
It uses your browser's built-in Chinese calendar plus a verified Lunar New Year table, so January and February birthdays get the right animal.
