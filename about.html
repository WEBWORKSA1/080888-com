Our mission

Numbers are culture. Culture moves money.

080888 explains how Chinese-speaking communities read numbers, and turns that into tools anyone can use to choose a phone number, price a product, pick a date or value an asset.

Why we exist

More than a billion people hear numbers as words. A price ending in 8 feels prosperous, and an address with a 4 feels risky. Those reactions show up in auction records, property prices and wedding calendars. Most of the web explains this as trivia. We treat it as practical knowledge for decisions.

What we believe

  • Respect first. We present traditions accurately and with context, not as exotic superstition.
  • Transparent models. Our scoring rules are published on each tool page.
  • No false promises. We explain how numbers are perceived. We don't guarantee luck.
  • Free core tools. Paid for by ads, sponsors, optional services and reader support.

How we make money

Advertising (including Google AdSense), sponsorships, optional paid reports and consultations, marketplace matchmaking fees, affiliate partnerships and reader donations. Sponsored content is always labelled.

Editorial standards

Facts about auctions, dates and events are sourced from public records and major news outlets. Found an error? Tell us and we'll fix it quickly.
