How Chinese families pick dates

  • Digits of the date. 8s (prosperity), 6s (smoothness) and 9s (lasting) help; 4s hurt. August, the 8th month, is popular.
  • Lunar days. Lunar days ending in 8 or 6 are welcome, and lunar days with a 4 are avoided.
  • Ghost Month. The 7th lunar month is avoided for weddings, moves and openings.
  • Romantic dates. 5/20 (520 “I love you”) and dates that spell 1314 (“a lifetime”) are popular for proposals and weddings.
  • The Tong Shu almanac (通书). Traditional selection also checks the day's clash animal against the couple's birth signs. Our hand-picked date service covers that.
