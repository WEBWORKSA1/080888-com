Years start on Lunar New Year, not 1 January. Lucky-number lists vary between traditions; these are the most widely published. Element numbers follow the He Tu (河图) pairing: Water 1/6, Fire 2/7, Wood 3/8, Metal 4/9, Earth 5/0.

The Five Elements (五行)

Each year also has an element (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water), set by the last digit of the lunar year. The 12 animals and 5 elements combine into a 60-year cycle. 2026 is a Fire Horse year; 2027 is a Fire Goat year.

The Great Race legend

In the classic story, the Jade Emperor held a race across a river. The clever Rat rode on the Ox's back and jumped ahead to finish first. That's why the zodiac starts with Rat, then Ox, and ends with the slow-but-happy Pig.
