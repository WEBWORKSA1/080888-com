The 15-day festival at a glance

  • Before New Year: clean the house to sweep away bad luck, hang red couplets and 福 (fortune) characters, settle debts.
  • New Year's Eve: the reunion dinner. Fish (鱼 yú ≈ 余 surplus) and dumplings (shaped like ingots).
  • Day 1: visit elders, give red envelopes (红包), wear red. No sweeping, which would sweep the luck out.
  • Day 5 (破五): welcome the God of Wealth. Many businesses reopen.
  • Day 15: Lantern Festival with lanterns, riddles and tangyuan.

Lucky numbers for the New Year

  • Red-envelope amounts: 88, 168, 188, 288, 888. Even numbers, no 4s.
  • Gift counts: pairs and 8s (8 oranges, 2 bottles). Never 4.
  • Business promos: “8.8% off”, “$88 bundle”, “168 lucky draw”.
  • Messages: 恭喜发财 (gōng xǐ fā cái, “wishing you prosperity”) uses the same 发 as the number 8.

Taboos to avoid

  • Breaking dishes (if you do, say 岁岁平安 “peace every year”)
  • Giving clocks, which sounds like attending a funeral
  • Giving scissors or knives, which cut ties
  • Lending money on day 1
