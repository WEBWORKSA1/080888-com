Feng shui and numbers

Feng shui is about how space, direction and timing affect wellbeing and prosperity. Numbers run through all of it: house and floor numbers, elements, and time cycles.

House and unit numbers

Practitioners usually look at the full number and the last digit. 8, 6, 9 and 1 are welcome. 4 is avoided. Some schools add the digits together (e.g. 1,628 → 1+6+2+8 = 17 → 1+7 = 8), so a “4” address can reduce to a better number.

Living with a 4 address

  • Put the number on a gold or red plaque.
  • Add a slightly rising line under the number (“moving up”).
  • Put healthy plants or running water near the entrance.
  • Most important for resale: know that Chinese-speaking buyers may discount the price, and plan for it.

Where numbers fit in a feng shui review

A full review covers the building's facing direction, floor plan, entrance, and the occupants' birth data (Kua number, BaZi). Numbers are one part of it. They matter most for signage, pricing, contact details and timing.
