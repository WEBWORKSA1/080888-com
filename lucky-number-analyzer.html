How the score works

  1. Digit weights. Each digit is scored by its Chinese sound-alike. 8 (发 prosper) is +10, 6 and 9 are +6, 2, 3 and 0 are mildly positive, 5 and 7 are neutral, and 4 (死 death) is −10.
  2. Endings matter more. The last four digits count 1.5×, because that's what people remember and say out loud.
  3. Named combinations. 27 phrases people recognise: 168 一路发, 518 我要发, 1314 一生一世, 520 我爱你 add points; 14 要死, 514 我要死, 748 去死吧, 250 二百五 subtract.
  4. Pattern value. Triples (AAA), repeats (ABAB) and solid lucky runs are easier to remember and worth more when resold.
